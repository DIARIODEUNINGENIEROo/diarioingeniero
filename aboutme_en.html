Diariodeuningeniero







Sergio López Martínez

Born in Madrid, he has lived most of his life in Guadalajara, a city he knows inside out. He studied at the public school Alcarria and later at the public high school Liceo Caracense, which is discussed on this website in the Guadalajara section, as it is a building with a great deal of history. He studied at the University of Alcalá, Degree in Telecommunications Engineering and subsequently the Official Master's in Advanced Electronic Systems. He worked at the University of Alcalá for a year and a half thanks to being awarded a training scholarship, and also as research staff during the final year of his Master's. The research scholarship involved adapting a complete project on a Virtex 5, using the schematics and designs from his Master's thesis project, to a Spartan 6, a lower-cost and smaller FPGA that would more than meet the client's requirements. While carrying out the research scholarship, he received another one from the Guadalajara city council to collaborate at Selekron, a local company specialized in electronic control equipment, with a presence in projects for well-known Spanish companies. Upon completing both scholarships, he embarked on a trip around Europe, starting in France. There, the stop was Toulouse, the capital of the European aerospace industry. He took a course at the Alliance Française to improve the French he had acquired years earlier at the Official School of Languages, with the aim of being able to find work. Once he had completed the first courses, he began working at a logistics company in the area, Adrexo, while continuing to perfect his French and studying English in some free courses available locally. Toward the end of the year, and seeing that obtaining a qualified job was quite difficult for someone with so little experience, he decided to head to a more dynamic country where changing jobs was the order of the day. Later he was in England, where he began learning English through free state courses or at local bookshops, as well as in parks, associations, and a paid academy in the area. In September of that same year he took the IELTS English certification exam and achieved a B2/C1. After four months gaining great proficiency in the language, he landed his first job at an electronic components factory in central England. A couple of months later he would join NSN (Nokia Siemens Networks). At NSN he worked on adapting a new radio module for all BTSs while maintaining all the improvements from the latest designs, doubling the band capacity. In late 2014 the company announced that it was going to move the project to India as part of its new strategic plan, in which costs were being reduced, offering various options to relocate the department's staff. Later he would return to Spain to work at Indra as a software development engineer, adapting a previous design so that it would comply with new regulations. This included adding certain functionalities, testing tasks and technical specification documentati In the meantime, he would take a 500-hour cybersecurity course as well as create this very website that you can now see. The course offers a fairly general approach but at the same time focuses on the practical use of different security tools to control the traffic that can enter and leave a network, and how more sophisticated tools — most of which are based on Kali Linux — work. Kali Linux is an operating system quite similar to Ubuntu, focused on these topics. Also covered is data analysis when receiving an attack, how to hide vulnerabilities in packets, or how to discover those packets. Upon completing the course, he was offered the chance to do an internship at a well-known company in Guadalajara, Orange, and they ended up hiring him as a telephone technical support operator, where he acquired social and commercial skills through customer service. That is where the website began, until he obtained a domain and linked everything together. Next year he traveled to the Canary Islands with the aim of getting to know the island for future study plans. In April he took the IELTS exam for a second time, renewing the previously achieved B2. A month later he returned to the peninsula to look for work and ended up working at Assystem, a French consultancy that works closely with Siemens. In June of that same year he started working, performing tester duties in the railway sector, taking part in the project to renovate the systems of Madrid's metro line 5. In August, after studying responsive web design, he decided to incorporate it into his website, beginning a complete renovation of this website using the classes provided by Bootstrap, specifically version 4. Due to his vocation as a teacher, he moved to Las Palmas, where he began the University Master's in Teacher Training for Compulsory Secondary Education and Sixth Form, Vocational Training, and Language Teaching, through the University of La Laguna and the University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. After completing the theoretical subjects, he carried out his teaching practice at the IES El Rincon school, a modern high school in the capital. In June he successfully presented his Master's thesis on a modern and innovative curriculum for students of the Computer Systems module of the Higher Vocational Training cycle in Multiplatform Systems, and he would begin working as a teacher in different parts of Spain. The Community of Madrid, Castilla La Mancha, and Andalusia.
